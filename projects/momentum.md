Movement in Cyberpunk 2077 keeps interrupting itself: a slide stops your sprint, a dash drops you to a walk, aiming slows you down, and every landing stuns you for a moment. Momentum removes those interruptions so one motion flows into the next.

What it changes: slide sideways, not only forward; keep sprinting through slides and dashes; aim without being forced to walk; a smoother dodge and dash; shorter landing stuns.

Published on Nexus Mods under the name saekoto on 28 October 2026. A few hundred downloads in the first days.
