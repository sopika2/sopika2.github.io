Persistent Corpses

2024 – 2025

A Cyberpunk 2077 mod so bodies stayed where they fell.

Night City cleans up after you very quickly. This mod stopped it, so the aftermath of a fight stayed on the ground instead of fading out when you looked away.

Published on Nexus Mods under the name saekoto.

Cause of death: a game patch, and I did not keep up with it.
